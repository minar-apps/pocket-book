Step 10

Keep it on your phone

  1. 1
    iPhone: open https://minar-apps.github.io/pocket-book/ in Safari, tap Share (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. 2
    Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. 3
    It now opens like an app. Answers you've opened work without internet; tap More → Save all for offline once on Wi-Fi to keep everything.
Updates come by themselves. When you're online the app always loads the newest version. If something looks old, close the app fully and open it again.
